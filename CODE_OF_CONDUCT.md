# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, publishing private information, or conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban participants for behavior inconsistent with this code. Report conduct privately through the maintainer contact configured in the GitHub repository. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporter privacy will be respected.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
